Games found

Installed through Steam, Epic, Riot, GOG, Ubisoft or the Xbox app. These are recorded even windowed; anything else only when it is fullscreen. Add any other program, Discord say, to record it too. A game being recorded stays recorded when you tab over to it.

What to record

Clips get the game, PC audio and your mic as separate tracks, plus a mix of them all; you pick which to keep when you cut.

Encoding

Used by the Shrink button. Keeps the resolution and frame rate, plays on Discord and the web, and uses your graphics card when it can. The original is kept if a clip would not get smaller.

After cutting

Off by default. With this off you are asked each time. There is no trash folder, so deletions are permanent.
